## Overview
The government agency wants to incorporate the chatbot into its mobile application using AI technology to enhance the availability of the services for the citizens. This system utilizes six fields of IT to guarantee reliability, security, and accessibility of the system. Software engineering is used in developing the interface for the chatbot and its connection with government systems. AI field helps the assistant in understanding the queries of citizens and providing the response from the information. Mobile computing is the ability of accessing the services via mobile phones. Networking makes sure that communication is done between the application and government servers. Cybersecurity ensures the safety of personal data of the citizens and the data management updates the service information.
